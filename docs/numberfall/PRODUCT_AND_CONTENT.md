# Product and content guide

## One-line positioning

Numberfall turns quick arithmetic into a vibrant swipe-to-solve puzzle: link neighbouring numbers and operators, hit the target, and keep the board—and your combo—moving.

## Product promise

Numberfall is approachable in seconds but rewards clever path-finding. It combines readable mental maths, arcade pace, satisfying tile movement, proof-backed targets, and short replayable sessions without requiring an account or internet connection.

## Audience

- Casual puzzle players who enjoy short, score-driven sessions.
- Players who like number games but want more motion and personality than a worksheet aesthetic.
- Adults, teens, families, and learners comfortable with basic arithmetic.
- Returning players motivated by combos, personal bests, difficulty options, and a Daily Challenge.

Do not market the game as formal education, exam preparation, cognitive treatment, or scientifically proven brain training.

## Core gameplay, in plain language

1. See a target from 1 to 99.
2. Start on a number tile.
3. Drag through neighbouring operator and number tiles to build an expression.
4. Match the target to clear the selected tiles.
5. Watch tiles fall and refill, then solve the next proven target.
6. Chain successes to grow the combo and score.

The full locked rules live in `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md`. Website copy can simplify the explanation, but must not contradict it.

## Confirmed features worth promoting

- Swipe through numbers and `+`, `−`, `×`, and `÷` operators.
- Eight-direction tile paths, including diagonal connections.
- Strict left-to-right arithmetic with whole, non-negative intermediate values.
- Standard games configurable to 1, 2, 3, or 5 minutes; three minutes is the default.
- Easy, Normal, and Hard target-selection preferences; Easy is the default.
- A fixed 60-second offline Daily Challenge with repeat practice and recent history.
- Combos, high scores, longer-expression rewards, and time bonuses for long solutions.
- Interactive first-launch tutorial and an optional solution hint in standard games.
- Original menu and gameplay music, sound effects, and optional haptics.
- High-contrast and reduced-motion settings.
- Local progress and preferences; no account or network is required for play.

## Message hierarchy

### Primary

**Build expressions. Beat the clock.**

Support line:

**Link numbers, solve targets, and make the board fall in a vibrant arithmetic puzzler.**

### Secondary messages

- **Swipe. Solve. Score.** Find a path, hit the target, and chase the next combo.
- **Every target has a way through.** Targets are selected from proven paths on the current board.
- **A fresh challenge every day.** Play the same deterministic Daily Challenge sequence for that date and ruleset, entirely offline.
- **Play at your pace.** Choose session length and difficulty, then use hints while learning.
- **Designed to stay readable.** Shape-distinct tiles, high contrast, and reduced motion support more ways to play.

Avoid saying every target has only one solution; uniqueness is not guaranteed.

## Voice and tone

- Energetic, concise, clever, and welcoming.
- Use active verbs: link, drag, solve, fall, build, chase, beat.
- Sound confident without hype that cannot be evidenced.
- Prefer short sentences and compact headings.
- Treat arithmetic as playful, not intimidating or remedial.
- Use British English to match existing copy (`maths`, not `math`) unless a localisation strategy deliberately changes locale.
- Use the real symbols `−`, `×`, and `÷`; do not reproduce mojibake visible in some console output.

## Claims that require future confirmation

Do not publish these until the project owner supplies evidence:

- “Available now”, a release date, or a functional store badge/link.
- iOS availability.
- “Free”, “ad-free”, or “no in-app purchases”.
- Age rating, supported Android versions, downloads, ratings, reviews, awards, or press quotes.
- Formal privacy/compliance statements beyond “no account or internet connection required for play”.
